Our Mission

Empowering Brilliant Minds
for a Better Tomorrow

Genius Model School exists to provide a world-class, holistic education that develops the intellectual, moral, technical, and creative abilities of every student — preparing them to be leaders in their community and Nigeria at large.

Located on Eziweka Road, Awada Obosi, Anambra State, we combine strong academic foundations with practical skills, digital literacy, entrepreneurship, and character development.

📚
Academic ExcellenceRigorous curriculum, experienced teachers, and modern learning facilities
💻
Digital & Technical SkillsICT labs, CBT centre, coding, and vocational workshops
🌟
Character & ValuesDiscipline, integrity, leadership, and community service
🚀
EntrepreneurshipCreative thinking, innovation, and business readiness

Our Vision

A Genius in Every Child

To be the leading model school in Anambra State — where every student discovers their genius, masters essential life skills, and graduates as a confident, ethical, and globally competitive individual.

Our Goals

What We Strive For

Through purposeful teaching, modern infrastructure, and community partnerships, Genius Model School delivers transformational education across four key areas.

01
🎓

Academic Excellence

Providing rigorous academic programmes across all subjects with experienced, qualified teachers and a structured, supportive learning environment that drives outstanding results in WAEC, NECO, and NABTEB.

02
💻

Digital Literacy & ICT

Equipping every student with computer skills, digital literacy, CBT readiness, and exposure to emerging technologies — preparing them for modern careers and examinations.

03
🔧

Technical & Vocational Training

Hands-on workshops in electrical, mechanical, automobile, catering, and construction technology that give students marketable skills for immediate employment or entrepreneurship.

04
🌍

Character & Leadership

Building integrity, discipline, teamwork, and community responsibility in every student — because true genius is not just academic but also moral and social.
